Founder & Creative Director

Lakshit Kumar is the founder of Shira Oni Interactive and the lead developer behind Shattered Dawn. With over 4 years of experience in programming and game development, he specializes in Unreal Engine 5, C++, gameplay systems, and technical design.

He leads every aspect of development—from gameplay programming and combat systems to level design, tools development, and creative direction—with a focus on building immersive action experiences that combine technical excellence with memorable gameplay.
